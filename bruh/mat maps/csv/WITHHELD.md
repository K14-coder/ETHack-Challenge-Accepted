# Withheld from this folder

`materiality_long.csv`, `materiality_wide.csv` and the 36 `raw/<TICKER>_ES.csv` /
`raw/<TICKER>_G.csv` dumps carry Bloomberg Terminal cell values and are not
shared, because they require the proper Bloomberg licensing. We are happy to
show you the files in person, to prove that the model is built on real data and
that the approach is scalable and feasible.

See `../WITHHELD.md` for the full manifest and for what remains here — the
schema, the coverage statistics, the field availability map, our own credit
workbook, and every computed output of the model.

The schema of `materiality_long.csv` is documented in full in `README.md` in this
folder, so the exact shape of the withheld data is public even though its values
are not.
