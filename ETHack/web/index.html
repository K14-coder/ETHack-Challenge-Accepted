Which choice decides it

Method. Specification curve analysis — Simonsohn, Simmons & Nelson, Nature Human Behaviour 4 (2020) — applied to corporate sustainability scoring. The nine axes are the forks where MSCI's and Bloomberg's published methodologies make different choices; see provider-decision-points.md for the citation behind each. Steps 1–5 run in model/spec.py and ship as a pillar cube; the last steps run in this page, so nothing here is precomputed for a particular answer.

Data. Bloomberg materiality maps for 18 S&P 500 energy companies across five BECS peer groups, 2021–2025, plus a FY2025 credit workbook built from SEC filings and agency adjustments. Nothing is imputed: a cell empty in the export is missing here. EQT and ONEOK have no export and are absent. There is no revenue and no headcount in the data, so EBITDA is the economic denominator — one rung below the fallback Bloomberg's own methodology already calls a fallback. The credit workbook is one fiscal year, so under change the financial pillar has no trajectory and drops out.
