# Demo and pitch

Owned by one person from hour 0. The pitch is a design constraint on the build, not a writeup of it.

## Pitch script

Target length from [[Efforts/Active/ETHack/challenge|challenge]]. Write it as spoken words, not bullets. Rehearse out loud, timed, twice.

**Hook (15s).** One concrete scene or number. No "in today's world".

> 

**Problem (30s).** Who hurts, how much, why existing answers fail.

> 

**What we built (30s).** Plain sentence, then straight into the demo.

> 

**Demo (90s).** See run sheet below. Narrate what the judge is seeing, do not narrate the architecture.

**Why it works (30s).** The one technical or design decision worth defending.

> 

**What is next (15s).** Honest, specific, small. Not "scale to 10 million users".

> 

**Close (10s).** Restate the hook resolved.

>

## Slides

Max slides from [[Efforts/Active/ETHack/challenge|challenge]]. One idea per slide, big type, no paragraphs.

1. Title: product name, one-line what it does
2. The problem, one number
3. The demo (live, slide is just a holding frame)
4. How it works, one diagram
5. What is next
